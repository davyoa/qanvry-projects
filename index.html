ABOUT US

PROVEN COMMITMENT TO QUALITY & INTEGRITY

Cladel Construction Limited is a registered construction and engineering company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992). We are committed to delivering innovative, high-quality and sustainable construction solutions that meet the needs of residential, commercial and institutional clients across Ghana.

With a strong reputation for professionalism, integrity and excellence, we provide comprehensive construction services from concept development and architectural design to project completion. Our experienced team of architects, engineers, project managers and skilled craftsmen work collaboratively to deliver projects that combine functionality, durability, and aesthetic appeal.

At Cladel Construction Limited, we understand that every project is an investment. That is why we are dedicated to delivering quality workmanship, maintaining the highest safety standards, and ensuring projects are completed on time and within budget. Whether you are planning a new building, renovating an existing property, or seeking expert construction consultancy, we are your trusted partner in bringing your vision to life.
